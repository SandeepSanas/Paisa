Lab trades

How the Lab works. The server runs all five ideas every minute on NIFTY and SENSEX, 9:16 am – 3:30 pm on market days, even when the app is closed. Paper only: nothing here places an order, and your signals, Options tab and AlgoBot are unchanged.
Same for all five: 5-minute candles · ATM strike of the nearest expiry · stop and targets from the index range, like the Options tab · entries 9:20 am – 2:45 pm (not after 1 pm on expiry day) · AlgoBot's exits (at T1 the stop moves to T1 − ₹5, then trails; everything closed at 3:15 pm) · one trade at a time per strategy per index · 1 lot · ₹165 a trade for charges and slippage.
1 TradeBot: the current option signal (index trend + PCR + OI change, confirmation meter), as a server copy. 2 RSI + MACD: RSI 14 at or below 25 in the last 4 candles, MACD below its signal line and the histogram rising 2 candles → Call; the mirror at 75 → Put. 3 Supertrend: a candle touches the Supertrend line and closes back on the trend side, or the line flips. 4 VWAP: a futures candle closes across VWAP with above-average volume. 5 Volume divergence: a new high on fading buying volume (distribution) → Put; a new low on fading selling volume (accumulation) → Call. Volume comes from NIFTY FUT / SENSEX FUT; volume on rising candles counts as buying, on falling candles as selling.
OI agreed / disagreed: at each entry the server notes whether TradeBot's OI reading pointed the same way. It never blocks a trade; it shows whether an OI filter would have helped.
Bitcoin and Gold run the same five ideas on Delta's futures, 24 hours a day. Column 1 follows the main TradeBot signal for that market; columns 2–5 use the market's own candles and volume. Same for all five: stop beyond the last 5 candles (the app's rule) but never tighter than 0.25% of the price, T1 at 1× and T2 at 2× the risk, half booked at T1 and the stop moved to entry, 2-hour time exit, 0.03 BTC / 1 XAUT, real Delta fees, results in rupees at ₹88 a dollar. There is no option-chain OI for these markets. Column 6, Anti-Gravity script (Bitcoin and Gold only), runs your TradingView script with its own rules, not the shared ones: stop about 2.85× ATR, half booked at 0.9× the risk, stop then moved just past entry, exits only when a 5-minute candle closes beyond its trailing stop, and a direct switch when the opposite signal comes.

NIFTY condor (paper, 1 lot): one weekly iron condor, opened at 10:00 am on the first market day with 4–8 days to expiry. Sell the call and put about 2.5% away from NIFTY, buy protection 400 points further out (worst case about ₹21,000–25,000). Close if NIFTY touches a sold strike, if the loss reaches 2× the credit, or at 12:00 noon on expiry day. Charges: ₹20 an order, STT 0.15% of sold premium, exchange fees, GST, stamp duty and ₹0.50 a unit slippage on every leg. Judge it after 8–12 weeks.

How signals work (v5)

On every closed candle: trend on the higher timeframe (EMA 21), EMA 9/21, VWAP, Supertrend (10, 3), RSI 14, ADX 14 and volume. A signal needs a trigger (Supertrend flip or 3-candle breakout), the higher-timeframe trend, and a volume spike wherever real volume exists. No signals when ADX is below 18 (chop), in the first 5 minutes of NSE, or after 3:00 pm IST.

Setup, then entry. A signal first shows as BUY SETUP / SELL SETUP with a price 0.6 ATR better than the signal candle's close. The trade opens only if price comes back to it within 3 candles; otherwise it's cancelled. In 60 days of testing on 13 markets this cut the average loss per trade from −0.15R to about −0.01R.

Stop = recent swing ± 0.1 ATR, kept between 0.8 and 2 ATR. T1 = 1R (half booked, stop moves to entry), T2 = 2R. Time exit after 24 candles (2 hours on 5-minute). STRONG = 5–6 of 6 checks agree, normal = 4 of 6. Educational tool, not investment advice.
