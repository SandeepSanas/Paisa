Lab trades

Rules. Buy (accumulation): RSI 14 fell to the chosen extreme within the last 6 candles; a heavy selling candle (2× average volume) appeared and selling volume then faded below 60% of it; the MACD histogram turned up for 2 candles or showed bullish divergence; entry when a candle closes above the previous candle's high. Stop below the lowest low of the fall; Target 1 at EMA 21 or VWAP; Target 2 at 2× risk; half is booked at T1. Sell (distribution) is the mirror image. Indices have no volume, so the volume steps are skipped for them. Sweep requires the move to have broken the previous 20-candle low/high and closed back inside. Active hours keeps crypto and gold to 1:30–10:30 pm IST (London + New York), US stocks to US hours and NSE to 9:30 am–3:00 pm. Educational test only.

How signals work

On every closed candle: trend on the higher timeframe (EMA 21), EMA 9/21, VWAP, Supertrend (10, 3), RSI 14, ADX 14 and volume. A signal needs a trigger (Supertrend flip or 3-candle breakout). No signals when ADX is below 18 (chop), in the first 5 minutes of NSE, or after 3:00 pm IST.

Stop = recent swing ± 0.1 ATR, kept between 0.8 and 2 ATR. T1 = 1R (stop moves to entry), T2 = 2R. The backtest line on each card replays these exact rules on recent candles. Educational tool, not investment advice.
